About Yiddish Labs

Yiddish Labs was created to give researchers, educators, and Yiddish-speaking communities access to professional-grade AI tools for free. Our mission is Yiddish language preservation through technology — digitizing oral history, building training data, and making Yiddish accessible to the next generation.

All Yiddish AI tools on this platform are powered by fine-tuned Whisper models trained specifically on Yiddish speech data, achieving far higher accuracy on Yiddish than any off-the-shelf multilingual model.
