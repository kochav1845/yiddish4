Yiddish and Hebrew — Related but Different

While Yiddish is written in Hebrew script and contains a significant Hebrew and Aramaic vocabulary layer (approximately 15–20% of Yiddish vocabulary derives from Hebrew/Aramaic), the two languages are linguistically distinct. Yiddish has German grammatical structure; Hebrew is a Semitic language. Direct translation from Yiddish to Hebrew requires understanding both systems — not just shared letters.

Yiddish Labs handles this complexity with an AI model that understands the semantic content of Yiddish speech and produces natural modern Hebrew (or Biblical Hebrew register, depending on context).
